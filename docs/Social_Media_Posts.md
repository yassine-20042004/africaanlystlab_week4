## 💼 1. LINKEDIN POST DRAFT

**Headline**: 🚀 Thrilled to complete Week 4 & Final Capstone for the FinTrust Digital Bank Experience Lab with AnalystLab Africa!

**Body**:
Over the past 4 weeks, I have had the incredible opportunity to build and deliver an end-to-end financial intelligence, predictive risk modeling, and digital support solution for **FinTrust Digital Bank**.

Here is a summary of what I developed and delivered across 5 key project tracks:

📊 **Data Analytics**:
- Built an interactive HTML executive dashboard and refined SQL queries using CTEs and Window Functions.
- Identified critical operational insights, including mobile app transaction friction (5.80% failure rate) and cross-border risk concentration (36.88% review flag rate).

🤖 **Data Science & ML Engineering**:
- Engineered 14 behavioural features and trained 4 candidate classification models (Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost).
- Selected Class-Weighted Logistic Regression as our production candidate, achieving **61.5% Recall** and **0.6730 ROC-AUC** for optimal risk detection.
- Containerized a FastAPI REST API inference service (`/predict`, `/health`) using Docker and Docker Compose.

💬 **Generative AI & Customer Support**:
- Built a zero-hallucination, knowledge-grounded customer support assistant.
- Implemented strict security guardrails that block credential exposure (PIN/OTP) and reject unauthorized financial advice requests.

🧪 **Validation & Quality Assurance**:
- Developed a 24-test automated Pytest suite achieving a 100% pass rate.

Huge thanks to **AnalystLab Africa** for this empowering internship experience!
